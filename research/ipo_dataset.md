# Mainboard IPO dataset (IPO track, step 1: data only)

**Data only. No strategy, no backtest, no trial.** `research/trials.jsonl` was not touched;
the DSR trial count stays at 21. Numbers are reported next to their sources; nothing here is
a judgement of the IPO track. The owner decides what comes next.

- **Built:** 2026-10-31, `scripts/build_ipo.py` -> `data/clean/ipo/ipos.parquet` (584 rows).
  Every table below is generated by `scripts/report_ipo.py` into
  `research/ipo_dataset_tables.md`; this file quotes it. Columns and rules: `docs/data.md`.
- **Universe:** one row per mainboard IPO whose bidding ran on NSE, bid opening 2016-01-01
  to 2026-10-31. "Year" is the calendar year of the bid close.
- **Holdout.** The table covers dates after `holdout_start` (2025-04-08). Per the owner's
  decision of 2026-10-31 (`research/phase2_literature.md`), the holdout is already
  contaminated for IPOs, and the IPO track's out-of-sample test is paper-tracking every
  new mainboard IPO from 1 Nov 2026. This step describes data and runs no backtest.
- **Tags:** PRIMARY = NSE (API, archives, bhavcopy, NSE's copy of the issuer's
  basis-of-allotment advert). UNVERIFIED = aggregator or news.

**No field comes from an aggregator.** The Tapetide pages (UNVERIFIED) are used only for
the cross-checks in section 4. So there are no aggregator-only fields to mark; the price
of that choice is the gap for 2016 to Sep 2018 below.

**Coverage by period:** NSE's advert archive starts in late 2018. The first advert found is
GRSE (bid close Sep 2018, file dated 9 Oct 2018); none was found for the 62 listed issues of
2016-2017 (9-14 dates tried each) or for the other 22 listed issues of 2018. From 2019 on,
483 of 485 listed issues have an advert (none found for Dodla Dairy 2021 or IREDA 2023
under any date tried). So for 2016 to Sep 2018 there is **no retail applicant count,
allottee count, allotment ratio, 1-lot chance or allotment date**. The exchange retail multiple and the
listing data are complete for those years.

**Pre-open price.** The listing-day open is the bhavcopy open. On an IPO's listing day NSE
runs a call auction before continuous trading, and the open is the price it discovered
(rule as reproduced from SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012; the circular was not read in
the original). Checked by hand against news for AU Small Finance Bank (2017): NSE opened at
530 (bhavcopy open 530), BSE at 525. Separately, that day's bhavcopy base price was 525,
not the 358 issue price; it is the only listing where NSE's list price lies in the band
but the base price does not, and the list price was used.

### 3.5 Overlapping windows and one Rs 15,000 block

Blocking interval per listed issue: from the bid open to the allotment date (the owner's
definition), and the shorter variant from the last bid day to the allotment date (applying
on the last day; any later start is never longer). Where the advert gives no allotment date
(all of 2016-2017, 22 of 2018, 47 later), the listing date ends the interval, which is
longer than the real block. Endpoints are inclusive: money released on a day is not reused
that day. "One block" is the most issues a single block can take one after another (greedy
by earliest end, which maximises the count). Every listed issue with a known lot fits the
block: one lot at the top of the band, less any retail discount, costs Rs 11,050 to
Rs 15,000 (median Rs 14,823).

| Year | Listed | Fit Rs 15,000 | Overlap another (full) | Max at once (full) | One block (full) | One block (last day) | Not coverable (full / last day) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 26 | 23 | 20 | 4 | 12 | 14 | 11 / 9 |
| 2017 | 36 | 33 | 30 | 5 | 15 | 16 | 18 / 17 |
| 2018 | 24 | 22 | 19 | 7 | 9 | 11 | 13 / 11 |
| 2019 | 16 | 15 | 8 | 3 | 11 | 11 | 4 / 4 |
| 2020 | 14 | 14 | 9 | 5 | 8 | 9 | 6 / 5 |
| 2021 | 63 | 63 | 61 | 8 | 21 | 26 | 42 / 37 |
| 2022 | 39 | 38 | 33 | 5 | 16 | 20 | 22 / 18 |
| 2023 | 57 | 56 | 49 | 8 | 22 | 29 | 34 / 27 |
| 2024 | 90 | 89 | 75 | 9 | 40 | 55 | 49 / 34 |

settles (T+1 after listing); withdrawn and BSE-only issues, which also block money while
bidding is open.

## 5. What is unverified or missing

- **2016 to Sep 2018 retail basis of allotment and allotment dates** (84 listed issues):
  not on NSE's advert archive. Not taken from other sources: BSE's IPO API refused scripted
  access, and the permission system blocked a retry as bot-check circumvention; Chittorgarh,
  the only aggregator seen with these tables, disallows AI crawlers in robots.txt (GPTBot,
  ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, CCBot; Claude is not named); IPO Central answered 403.
  Lead-manager and registrar websites were not searched issue by issue.
- **40 adverts from 2019 on that cannot be read** (37 unreadable, 3 without a findable
  retail section). OCR was not tried: it needs a new tool (e.g. Tesseract) in the project.
- Valid applications for 87 of the 443 readable adverts from 2019 on (73: no TOTAL line
  fits; 14: no usable 1-lot row).
- Pre-open price = bhavcopy open rests on the call-auction rule as reproduced, plus one
  hand check (AU 2017). NSE does not publish a separate historical pre-open file that was
  found.
- BSE-only status of Protean, NSDL and NSE (inference from NSE data plus Tapetide);
  GreenSignal Bio, Dinesh Engineers and K.P.R. Agrochem as withdrawn or failed (no NSE
  listing found; no withdrawal notice read).
- Retail discounts exist only where an advert states them (5 issues); pre-Oct 2018 PSU
  discounts are not recorded.
- The exchange retail multiple is before rejections; the advert's is after; they differ
  (Bajaj Housing Finance 7.04 and 7.54). `retail_sub` uses the advert's where there is one.
- The 10% floor on the 1-lot row's share of shares applied, and the [0.9/x, 6/x] bound on
  the 1-lot chance, were set from this data (parsed adverts: 5th percentile 46%, median
  72%); they are sanity filters, not published rules.

## 7. Owner's decisions (2026-10-31, after reading this report)

1. **2016 to Sep 2018.** The 84 issues without allotment data stay as gaps. Chittorgarh is
   not used (its robots file is respected). The IPO backtest starts in 2019.
2. **Unreadable adverts** stay flagged. In the backtest, an issue with no parsed 1-lot chance
   gets p = min(1, k / retail_sub_exch), where k is the median average-lots-per-application
   of the parsed issues in the same year, and is marked as estimated. Results are also
   reported without these issues.
3. **Blocking.** A non-allotted application blocks money from the day we apply until
   1 working day after the allotment date. An allotted one blocks it until the
   listing-day sale settles and the money is back in the bank: listing date + 2 working
   days. Withdrawn issues count as choices (blocked through the bid window, zero return),
   because nobody knew in advance they would be withdrawn. BSE-only listings (CAMS,
   Protean, NSDL, NSE) are excluded for lack of BSE prices and are listed in the report.

Open for step 2, not decided here: which calendar "working day" means (NSE trading days
are the only calendar in the repo; bank holidays differ), and which inputs define k
(the advert's TOTAL row is after rejections, `retail_sub_exch` is before).
